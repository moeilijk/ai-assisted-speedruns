# Portal 2

You are playing Portal 2 through SourceAutoRecord's TAS protocol. The game is loaded and paused before your first
action, and it only moves when you tell it to.

- `p2.observe()` tells you where you are: the map, the tick, and the player's position, angles and velocity.
- `p2.script(text)` plays an inline `.p2tas` script. Begin it with `start now` so it continues from here.
- `p2.advance(n)` steps n ticks without input, `p2.pauseAtTick(t)` stops playback at a tick.
- There is no screenshot: you see the game through what you read, not through a picture.

Play the run to the goal you were given. Nothing else in the game is yours to change: no console commands, no
cheats, no settings.
