# Choosing the seed for an AI run (Slay the Spire)

The reference seed for the Ironclad act3 category is **`23M`** (2577), chosen on measured winnability: the simulator's whole-run agent wins it in 67 of 96 playouts (70%) against 6% on random seeds (see "Winnability, measured"). `2PL` (3311) came first on the nine paper criteria and was the reference seed for two hours that evening; the measurement rejected it (0 wins in 48 playouts) and it is kept here as the example of why the checklist alone is not enough. The seed is part of the category set-up: every model plays the same map, encounters, rewards and events, so runs are comparable, and the seed goes into `summary.json` (`seed`, `attempts`) for reproduction. Seeds are not picked at random: the community searches them with tools (ForgottenArbiter's SeedSearch mod, gamerpuppy's sts_seed_search) and has proven that some seeds cannot be won at all (OohBleh, seed `18ISL35FYK4`, Silent A18+: a 144 HP Lagavulin as the only floor-6 fight, three fights before it, no rest or shop, only cards without damage scaling). A seed chosen without checking is a run lost before it starts.

## What a seed depends on (all of it must be recorded)

- **Game version**: card and relic pools changed between patches; speedrun.com lists the version next to every seeded run. Ours: V2.3.4 (12-18-2022), in `category.build`.
- **Unlock level per character**: unlocks add cards and relics to the pools, so the same seed gives different rewards at another unlock level (runners write "Unlocks: 2501-321" next to their seed). This profile: Ironclad 5 (everything unlocked), Silent 1. To record in `game-config/` at publish (from `preferences/STSUnlockProgress`), not done yet.
- **Ascension**: 0 for AI runs (`AAS_STS_ASCENSION`).
- **Neow's options** are reported to depend on how the previous run ended (forum: "provided your last run reached the first boss"); to be measured across our own restarts before we rely on "same seed = same start".

## Criteria for an AI seed

The player is an LLM with the full game state, no time pressure between commands, no memory beyond its context, and (measured with a naive bot on this install) an early death rate that is all about act 1 elites: our stub runs died at floor 6 (Lagavulin, twice), floor 6 (3 Sentries), floor 7 (Gremlin Gang), floor 13 (Gremlin Nob). The seed must give a competent, non-expert player a fair chance, not a sprint route.

1. **Class and pool**: Ironclad (the most direct class: strength, block, big attacks), ascension 0, unlock level 5. SeedSearch: `playerClass: IRONCLAD`, `ascensionLevel: 0`, `ironcladUnlocks: 5`.
2. **Neow**: Neow's Lament must be on offer (enemies in the first three fights have 1 HP): a safe opening and three free rewards. SeedSearch: `forceNeowLament: true`. The boss-relic swap is a gamble and is not chosen.
3. **Act 1 map**: a path exists with at most one elite before floor 9 and none on floor 6 (the floor-6 elite is what kills naive play), with a rest site before the act boss, and a shop reachable with card removal before floor 10. SeedSearch routes by the lowest summed room weight (Dijkstra; measured: with `eliteRoomWeight: 0.3` the route hunted elites and only 2 of 300 seeds passed `maximumElites: 1`), so: `eliteRoomWeight: 3.0` (an elite on the route is then a forced one), `restRoomWeight: 0` (rests are free), `shopRoomWeight: 0.6`, `maximumElites: 1`; the floor-6 rule, the rest before the boss and the shop are checked on the printed route by `seed-pick.mjs`.
4. **Early cards**: the first three card rewards must contain at least one straightforward damage or block card from this list, so a simple deck works: Pommel Strike, Shrug It Off, Iron Wave, Cleave, Thunderclap, Uppercut, Carnage, Inflame, Battle Trance, Feel No Pain, Whirlwind, Immolate, Bludgeon. Nothing that needs a combo to work (no Rampage-only, no Dual Wield, no Corruption without exhaust support). SeedSearch: `requiredAct1Cards` with one or two of these (the mod requires every listed card, so keep the list short and run several searches), the rest by reading the output.
5. **Act 1 relic**: at least one plain combat relic from the elite or the shop: Bag of Marbles, Vajra, Anchor, Red Skull, Bronze Scales, Oddly Smooth Stone, Blood Vial, Lantern, Orichalcum. SeedSearch: `requiredAct1Relics` (same caveat), otherwise read the output.
6. **Bosses**: act 3 not Time Eater (it punishes exactly what an LLM does: many small cards per turn); act 2 preferably not The Champ. Act 1 any. SeedSearch prints the bosses; filter by reading.
7. **No glitches, no shortcuts**: the run is played straight. The Secret Portal event (act 3, offered when the game's own clock is long, which is always the case for an AI) skips to the act 3 boss and would make runs incomparable: not taken. Decided forbidden on three layers: the rule in AGENTS.md, the plugin refuses to enter it (a milestone logs that it was offered), and the seed search runs with `speedrunPace: true` so the route never counts on it. Pandora's Box tricks and the smoke-bomb finish are not used.
8. **Length**: acts 1 to 3 (`act4: false`, `highestFloor: 51`); the Heart is a separate goal.
9. **Winnable, checked, not assumed**: a candidate seed is accepted only after (a) SeedSearch's printed route satisfies 2 to 6, (b) a scripted baseline on the real game (the stub with a simple heuristic) reaches at least the act 1 boss on it, and (c) the run history the game writes (`save-track.mjs`) confirms seed, path and bosses. Measured for 2PL (a scripted baseline run with the sts_lightspeed oracle): (a) passes, (b) the act 1 boss fell at 2:04 IGT without a death, (c) the game's own history reads `seed 3311, defeat by Automaton at floor 33, clock 0:07:26, path M?MM$RMRTRM??MRBM$MMMRM?TRMMMMRB`. The bot lost at the act 2 boss with 24 cards (still four Strikes and four Defends), 64/86 HP and three empty potion slots: that is the bot's out-of-combat heuristic (15 cards taken against 2 skipped, two removals, seven of seven rest sites spent on upgrading, potions drunk in ordinary fights), not the seed. Acts 2 and 3 are therefore not proven winnable on this seed by a baseline. The seed, version, unlocks and this checklist go into the game plugin's README.

## Search plan

1. Install ForgottenArbiter's SeedSearch (`seedsearch.jar` in `<game>/mods`, run headless with ModTheSpire); it writes `searchConfig.json`, to be filled from `seed-search.config.json` here (draft, keys from the mod's `SearchSettings.java`).
2. Search a range (the mod does about 40 seeds/s on this machine, so tens of thousands per run), then `node games/slay-the-spire/seed-pick.mjs <log>` scores every printed seed on criteria 2 to 6 and lists the candidates with the seed code the game uses.
3. Baseline the best three on the real game with the stub, record the history files, choose one.
4. Fix it: `AAS_STS_SEED=<code>` in `.env.example` as the reference seed for the Ironclad act3 category, seed and unlocks in the README, the Secret Portal rule in AGENTS.md.

Reference points from speedrun.com's Any% Seeded list (fixed seed list, class and version per run): Ironclad `UPG42` (1:54 on V2.3.4), Silent `14Y6HRUMBY` (1:26 on 2.3), Defect `7L7LGE` (1:59 on 2.2), Watcher `50IDN` (1:51 on 2.2). Those are sprint routes for humans with practice; they show what a searched seed looks like, they are not the AI reference seed.
